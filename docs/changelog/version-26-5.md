# Version 26.5

This update to the Live environment is scheduled for October 7, 2026 at 8:00 PM MDT.

Version 26.5 is a major release. It updates 116 pages across the platform, along with several shared components used throughout CMDS. These notes cover only the pages and features used by CMDS.

## Highlights

- The compliance summary report now shows up-to-the-minute information
- A new compliance trend report charts each department's compliance month by month, with Excel and PDF export
- Training reports offer new filter criteria and clearer labels
- Learners can request enrolment in a training program, and members of the CMDS Program Enrollment Reviewers role review each request and approve or reject it
- SCORM courses packaged in more than one language are supported, and course completion is reported more reliably from the Scoop (OpenSCORM) platform

## Behind-the-scenes improvements

This release also improves shared components that appear on many pages. You may notice small changes in these areas:

- Training report criteria and department user status criteria
- Compliance summary report results and the compliance workbook
- Executive summary on achievement status
- Person, department, and division details on CMDS pages
- Person and department lookups on CMDS pages
- Achievement lists, achievement status selection, and sign-off
- Competency validation lists and validation search results
- Invoicing teams
- Department and follower subscriber lists for CMDS messages, and notification settings on person records
- Education and training certificates and search results
- Admin navigation, including recent links
- Date and time selection, time zone lists, and time zone handling (Alberta adopted permanent daylight time (UTC−6) under the Official Time Act, eliminating the usual fall back on November 1, 2026)
- Person search criteria and results, memberships, and sign-in details
- Department checklists and role lists on groups
- Course outlines, activity setup, and course enrolment lists
- Program publication settings, contacts, and learner lists
- Class setup, location details, registration lists, and class search results
- Class registration reports and search downloads
- Achievement and credential details, lists, and search results
- Change history, and dashboard charts and grids
- Assessment question search, question printing, and form details
- Answering questions and viewing results in exams and quizzes
- Training plans and certificate lists on the learner portal
- Achievements, registrations, and search on the portal contact pages
- Navigation and search on the newer admin home page

If you have questions about anything in this release, reach out to CMDS Administration (<admin_cmds@keyera.com>).
